# Alaska Ice Crystals Living Site — Asset Manifest

This file is a continuity/protection record for approved or reserved OSKO-created assets.

## Hard rules
- Do not replace approved OSKO assets with generic internet images.
- Do not redraw or reinterpret a locked asset unless Osko explicitly asks.
- Add assets one at a time and record placement/behavior before moving to the next asset.
- Preserve a known-good checkpoint before major changes.

## Reserved living-world locations
- Gate / entrance structure — reserved for approved OSKO gate design.
- Cattle guard and fence — reserved for approved OSKO cattle guard/fence assets.
- SKIE 27 — reserved for approved three-track truck / CB / calculator concept.
- Headquarters — reserved for approved Alaska Ice Crystals main building.
- Aurora — reserved for Aurora's real approved appearance; no generic dog substitute.
- Workshop — reserved for approved OSKO workshop asset.
- Campfire — reserved for approved fire/pot/people gathering asset.
- Eagles — reserved for approved eagle assets at entrance and in sky.
- Wolves and other animals — reserved for approved OSKO animal assets.

## Current code-only placeholders
The v0.2–v0.3 gate, road, mountain and node graphics are structural placeholders only. They may be replaced locally by approved assets, but the rest of the world and navigation engine must remain intact.

## Workflow
1. Identify the approved asset.
2. Add it to the repository under `assets/`.
3. Record its exact filename here.
4. Place it without moving unrelated locked pieces.
5. Test on phone in Chrome.
6. Make a checkpoint branch after approval.
