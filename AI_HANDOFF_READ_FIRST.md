## 6. ROBOT PROGRAM — CURRENT DESIGN DIRECTION

Current team concept includes multiple human-like service robots, including male and female robots, designed to look like the approved concept images while remaining practical and serviceable internally.

- human-like proportions,
- articulated head, neck, shoulders, arms, elbows, wrists, hands, spine, hips, legs, knees, ankles, feet, fingers and toes,

## 8. ALASKA WINTER OPERATION

Robots are intended for Alaska use.

Plan automatic zoned thermal management rather than blindly heating the entire robot. Critical zones may include batteries, computers, cameras/sensors, actuators/joints, storage and other cold-sensitive systems.

Possible design features include:
- automatic temperature sensors,
- automatic heaters/warmer elements,
- insulated battery/electronics compartments,
- cold-rated lubricants/materials,
- ice/snow protection around joints and feet,
- warm-up mode before heavy movement,
- automatic power reduction or movement limits if critical components are too cold.

If Osko has a house, robots are intended to come inside at night, charge/dock, dry off, warm up, run diagnostics and enter low-power standby. They are not intended to live outside overnight when indoor shelter is available.
