# AI CONTINUITY RULES — ALASKA ICE CRYSTALS LIVING WEBSITE

## READ BEFORE CHANGING ANYTHING

This is not a normal website template. Alaska Ice Crystals is an original custom living 3D world built from Osko's plans, life experiences, designs, products, systems, and approved project material.

### Permanent rules

1. **Do not clean up the project structure.** Important source material is intentionally scattered across Drive, images, chats, code, and other records.
2. **Do not reorganize, consolidate, rename, move, or delete source material** unless Osko explicitly approves it.
3. **If something is missing or unclear, ask Osko for the missing piece.** State exactly what is missing and what you are trying to determine.
4. **Do not invent missing designs.** Do not substitute an internet design because it looks similar or seems easier.
5. **Internet research is for facts, laws, compatibility, regulations, and technical research only unless Osko explicitly approves creative source material.**
6. **Never delete something because you do not understand why it exists.** It may be intentional or reserved for future function.
7. **No major destructive rewrite on the only working copy.** Preserve a checkpoint first.
8. **Use three protection levels:** MASTER SAFE, CURRENT WORKING, TEST.
9. **Build in small controlled passes.** Check the result, keep what works, then continue.
10. **Objects may have future function.** Buildings, tools, vehicles, trains, aircraft, animals, stores, books, merchandise, and other objects are not automatically decoration.
11. **Ask specific questions instead of asking Osko to explain the entire project again.**
12. **Osko is the index.** Do not create one master document that reveals the location of every scattered source component.
13. **A new AI/model must treat prior approved work as existing project state, not disposable context.**
14. **If unsure, preserve first and ask before editing.**

## Website build protection

- `MASTER SAFE` = protected approved checkpoint.
- `CURRENT WORKING` = latest good build.
- `TEST` = experimental changes.

Promote changes upward only after they are confirmed good. Never erase the last known good build while experimenting.

## World architecture principle

The Alaska Ice Crystals site is a living, expandable 3D world. The current developed property is one region, not the final boundary. Architecture must remain expandable.

## Missing-piece examples

Good: `I found the truck exterior requirements but not the approved interior. Where should I look?`

Good: `I found the book system but not Aurora's approved artwork. Which record should I check?`

Bad: replacing the missing piece with something found online.

Bad: deleting a system because its purpose is not obvious yet.
