# OSKO / ALASKA ICE CRYSTALS — OS FOUNDATION — READ FIRST

**STATUS: PERMANENT PROJECT-DIRECTION RECORD — SEPTEMBER 11, 2026**

## THIS IS AN OS, NOT A WEBSITE DEMO

The Alaska Ice Crystals “Living Layover” is the living visual/control layer of Osko’s own operating system. It is not merely a website, launcher skin, wallpaper, game, or static scene.

The phone, Living Layover, school/library, files/tools, SKIE voice layer, future foldable-phone view, future 3D/spatial-glasses view, and the four robots are different views/devices connected to the SAME OS environment.

## DO NOT REDESIGN IT AS MICROSOFT WINDOWS OR LINUX

The final OS is being built from the ground up for OSKO / Alaska Ice Crystals. Do not default to a conventional desktop model with taskbars, nested menus, pop-up windows, file-browser-first navigation, mouse-first workflows, or endless click/scroll sequences.

Outside operating systems, frameworks, build tools, hosting, Android, GitHub, and other development platforms may be used temporarily to build/test/research/compile/host the project. They do not define the final interaction model or final OS architecture.

## THE WORLD IS THE INTERFACE

Direct interaction is the design language.

- The truck is the truck control.
- The shop is the shop control.
- HQ is HQ.
- The school is the school/library.
- A robot is that robot’s control/status point.
- Aurora, lake, gate, tower, fuel/maintenance, and other world objects can have their own actions/state.

Touching an object, speaking to it, and later selecting it in a spatial/3D view must map to the SAME underlying object/action/state system. Do not build separate incompatible touch, voice, AI, phone, glasses, and robot control systems.

## FIRST REAL BUILD MUST BE SUBSTANTIAL

Do not give Osko a tiny coded sketch, a few labels, or one small feature and expect him to understand or approve the OS concept.

The first real build must contain enough of the Alaska Ice Crystals property to judge the system as a living OS environment, including the recognizable land/property and major areas such as the gate/sign/cattle guard, roads, HQ, shop/workshop, truck yard, school/library, robot area/garage, lake/outdoor area, fuel/maintenance/communications areas as appropriate, Osko’s custom truck/trailer, Aurora, at least one robot, and enough living motion to judge the feel.

The property/world must move under the finger. One-finger drag must pan naturally. Pinch must zoom. Drag across an object must pan rather than accidentally open it; a deliberate tap activates the object. HOME/RESET must return to a useful property view.

## HIGH-CLASS / MODERN / LIVING VISUAL DIRECTION

The environment must feel high-class, professional, modern, different, and alive. It must not look like a flat coding test or static diagram.

Buildings must exist as scene objects that work in daylight and darkness, not as “night-only pictures.” The world is intended to follow real time:

- sunrise / morning light,
- sun movement through the day,
- afternoon,
- dusk,
- nighttime,
- moon and stars,
- interior/exterior lights,
- snow/ice response,
- weather,
- smoke/fire,
- animals,
- Aurora,
- robots,
- other environmental motion.

Build the world once and let time, lighting, weather, and state change the same world instead of swapping unrelated flat scenes.

## PHONE NOW, FOLDABLE + GLASSES LATER

Current minimum phone target recorded September 11, 2026:

- Samsung Galaxy A36 5G
- model SM-S366V
- Android 16 / One UI 8.5
- 6 GB physical RAM
- RAM Plus enabled

Make this phone usable and smooth, but do not let its small screen permanently define the OS.

Later, the same OS environment must expand to a foldable phone with more usable space and higher visual detail. After that, it should support a private spatial/3D-glasses view with voice, hand interaction, floating displays, virtual keyboard/pointer, and robot control.

## VISUAL QUALITY / PERFORMANCE

Use high-quality/high-resolution source assets where useful. “4K-quality” does not mean forcing every live frame to maximum resolution regardless of hardware. The system should adapt scene detail, shadows, reflections, distant objects, texture detail, and effects so the current phone stays smooth while better hardware automatically receives more detail.

Smooth interaction is mandatory. Do not trade usability for a marketing number.

## VOICE-FIRST DIRECTION

Voice is a primary interaction method, not an afterthought. A direct command such as “open the shop,” “show the truck,” “take me to the school,” “show Robot Four,” or “go home” should call the same action that a direct touch would call.

A small AI layer may later make natural wording smoother and more flexible, but deterministic actions and safety-critical functions must not depend solely on AI.

## OS OBJECT / ACTION FOUNDATION

Major world objects need identities, state, permissions, and actions underneath the visuals. This foundation is what allows touch, voice, AI assistance, foldable-phone controls, glasses/spatial interaction, and later robot interaction to stay compatible.

Do not hard-wire the project as a pile of unrelated buttons and pages.

## ROBOT SAFETY / BUILD ORDER

The Living Layover/OS is the proving and learning layer BEFORE physical robot control.

Robot safety-critical functions must remain local/deterministic and separate from higher-level AI where appropriate, including:

- motor limits,
- balance/stability control,
- emergency stop/shutdown,
- battery protection,
- thermal protection,
- sensor handling,
- safe degraded mode,
- allowed work zones,
- communications-loss behavior.

Do not experiment for the first time on a physical robot with behavior that can be tested safely in the OS/simulation layer first.

## DESIGN AUTHORITY

Osko is the designer and controls final design decisions. AI/developers help with engineering, coding, testing, technical amounts/limits, memory/performance, safety, compatibility, and explanation.

If an idea cannot or should not be implemented exactly as first described, explain WHY and offer the closest workable path. Do not silently replace the idea with a familiar Microsoft/Linux pattern.

## PRESERVE-FIRST RULE

Existing approved versions, visuals, handoffs, branches, builds, screenshots, and project history are evidence. Preserve them. Do not delete, overwrite, simplify, rename, move, or redesign project work merely because an AI does not understand it.
