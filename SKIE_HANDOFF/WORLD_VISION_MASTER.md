## CORE IDEA

This is not meant to become a normal website with pages and buttons. It is a living interactive world that visitors enter through the Alaska Ice Crystals gate. The visitor should feel like they are physically inside the world, not browsing a store page.

The world is intended to keep growing for years. New roads, buildings, stores, homes, jobs, animals, vehicles, activities, weather, games, tools and systems can be added over time without rebuilding the foundation.

The world is based heavily on Osko's real life experience, especially trucking, Alaska, work, safety, survival, equipment, animals, business and everyday consequences. Do not flatten this into a generic game concept.

## SAFETY THROUGH CONSEQUENCES

A major part of Osko's world is teaching safety through realistic consequences during play instead of constantly displaying classroom-style warnings.

Example: semi-truck tire repair.
- character has a flat
- character finds and uses a jack
- choices may include a proper jack stand and unsafe alternatives such as a log
- if the truck is not properly secured or the parking brake is not set, the truck can move/fall
- unsafe support may fail
- character can be injured
- ambulance/hospital consequences can follow
- the unfinished truck remains a problem the character must later return to and fix correctly

The point is for players to learn why safe procedures matter through the simulation.

Other safety scenarios may include road work/flagging and driving too fast, equipment use, hunting, work sites and other real-life situations.

Important design rule: do not intentionally harm bystander characters simply for shock value. Safety events should teach cause/effect and remain appropriate for the intended audience.

## SECURITY / PROTECTION DIRECTION

Security is essential because the world and its assets are intended to be valuable and difficult to copy.

Desired protections include:
- user account/email access for more advanced areas
- terms/agreements before entering advanced systems
- watermark/ownership marks throughout the world
- logging suspicious tampering attempts
- keeping one attacker's session from disrupting everyone else
- server-side ownership of important game/business logic
- asset protection and compartmentalized systems
- graceful failure on the attacker's/session's side rather than the whole world going down

Important: security must defend OSKO systems. Do not build retaliation that damages a user's computer, files, network or device. A hostile session may be blocked, disconnected, rate-limited or denied access, but the site must not attack the user's machine.
