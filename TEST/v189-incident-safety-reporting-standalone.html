ALASKA ICE CRYSTALS SCHOOL — V189 INCIDENT + SAFETY REPORTING
SAFETY EVENT RECORD

Record near-misses, training incidents, equipment damage, injuries, unsafe conditions and corrective actions so the school learns from events instead of losing the information.

REPORT STRUCTURE
Event type + date/time + training area
REQUIRED
Student / instructor / equipment involved
IDENTIFY
What happened + immediate response
DOCUMENT
Photos / evidence / witness information
ATTACH
Root-cause review + corrective action + follow-up
CLOSE LOOP
Production incident handling must distinguish emergencies from administrative reporting, protect medical/private information, preserve evidence appropriately, meet applicable reporting duties and use defined escalation and corrective-action procedures.
