EMERGENCY + CLOSURE PLAN
Rule: protect people first.

Weather, fire, earthquake, power loss, unsafe yard conditions, equipment failure or another serious hazard can stop training immediately.

1. STOP TRAINING
Instructor or authorized school lead can suspend activity when conditions are unsafe.
2. ACCOUNT FOR PEOPLE
Students, staff, visitors and anyone in yard/simulator/classroom areas.
3. SECURE EQUIPMENT / AREA WHEN SAFE
Do not create additional risk just to save equipment.
4. COMMUNICATE STATUS
Closure, delay, relocation, pickup/release instructions and rescheduling.
5. DOCUMENT + REOPEN ONLY AFTER REVIEW
Record cause, actions, unresolved hazards and authorization to resume.
PROTOTYPE POLICY

Production emergency planning must be matched to the actual property, building/yard hazards, local emergency requirements, fire/life-safety systems, staff roles, communications systems, accessibility needs and insurance/operating policies.
