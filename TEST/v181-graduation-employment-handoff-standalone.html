ALASKA ICE CRYSTALS SCHOOL — V181 GRADUATION + EMPLOYMENT HANDOFF
FROM TRAINING TO WORK

Graduation should not be a dead end. This layer prepares the student record for the next step: licensing completion where applicable, employer-ready skills summary, job placement support and post-graduation follow-up.

1 — Confirm completion gate
Curriculum, required hours, retraining, assessments and approvals verified.
2 — Build graduate skills summary
Document demonstrated strengths, specialized training and remaining restrictions honestly.
3 — Licensing / testing handoff
Route student to required external licensing or road-test process where applicable.
4 — Employer handoff
Prepare an employer-facing training summary with student authorization.
5 — Placement + follow-up
READY FOR NEXT STAGE
Prototype only. The school must not promise licensure, employment or regulatory acceptance. Production placement records, consent, disclosures and employer communications require verified policy and applicable law.
