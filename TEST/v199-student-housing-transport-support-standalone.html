ALASKA ICE CRYSTALS SCHOOL — V199 HOUSING + TRANSPORT SUPPORT
REMOVE NON-ACADEMIC BARRIERS

Some students will need help getting to training or finding short-term housing. The school needs a separate support track so those barriers do not get mixed into grades.

Transportation needCommute, shuttle, ride coordination, weather backup.
Temporary housing needReferral, availability, move-in/out dates, emergency backup.
Distance-student planningTravel dates, orientation timing, required in-person blocks.
Support statusOpen, arranged, waiting, resolved.
Privacy boundarySupport information should not alter academic evaluation.
Production support should use approved partners, written eligibility rules, privacy controls and clear limits on what the school does or does not provide.
